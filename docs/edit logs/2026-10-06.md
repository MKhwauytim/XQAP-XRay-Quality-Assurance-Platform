## v151.2 — 2026-10-06 — Fix (reports): comprehensive report renumbers sections, drops the population rail tab, supports deck v3, and states its study period

**Why:** v151.1 left three gaps on the «تقرير تنفيذي شامل» deck, and the owner asked for all of them to be fixed. (1) The sections kept their numbers, so the deck went المعجم → القسم الثاني → القسم الثالث. (2) The printed side rail still had a «مجتمع الفحص» tab. (3) Deck v3 did not handle the completed-only scope, so the page always opened v2. The owner also asked that the report state the period its samples cover: it printed «جميع_الأشهر».

**What changed:**
- **Deck v2:** `slideKit` gains a build-scoped omitted-section set (`setActiveOmittedSections`, set and reset by `buildDeckV2Slides` inside the existing build lock), plus `sectionNumber` / `navSectionLabel`. For the completed-only scope (sections 1 and 4 omitted) these drive:
  - the separators' shown number (slide ids and style-choice keys keep the fixed number);
  - the TOC titles («القسم الأول — نتائج فحص الجودة», «القسم الثاني — التحاليل المتقدمة»);
  - `data-section-label` and the slide eyebrows equal to it;
  - the side rail, which drops the omitted «مجتمع الفحص» tab.

  With nothing omitted, every helper returns the previous output, so the full-scope golden snapshots are unchanged.
- **Deck v3** honours `model.scope === "completed-only"`:
  - section 1 is skipped (TOTAL and the contents ranges are recomputed);
  - sections 2 and 3 are renumbered الأول/الثاني;
  - the cover gets a «تقرير شامل» kicker and a completed-samples cell;
  - the KPI slide becomes «المؤشرات الرئيسية», with completed / correct-suspicion / false-suspicion counts in place of population / sample / coverage;
  - the risk-level slide drops the draw weights and the monthly target, and shows the completed count and scope note instead;
  - the glossary drops «التغطية»;
  - month wording becomes report wording.
- **Comprehensive page:** reads the workspace's deck-edition preference, like the Reports tab, and opens v3 or v2 accordingly.
- **Study period:** `mergeCompletedRows` returns `period` (the earliest and latest normalized month among the completed rows, from system months and the workbook's «الشهر»). `buildComprehensiveInput(rows, base, period)` sets the new optional `ExecutiveReportInput.periodLabel` («من {from} إلى {to}», or the single month). `reportModel.periodId` and the document, deck v2 and deck v3 headers use it before the month-folder label. The page shows «فترة العينات: …» once rows are loaded. New labels: `ce_period_range`, `ce_period_title`. Rows carry only the day of month, not a full date, so the period is a month range.
